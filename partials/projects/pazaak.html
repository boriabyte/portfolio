Pazaak Closed Source In Progress

Recreation of mini-game featured in two Star Wars games, Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic & Knights of the Old Republic II: The Sith Lords. The application seeks to use OOP paradigm and practices (encapsulation, inheritance, occasional overloading, overriding) for the overall architecture. All the assets are handcrafted utilizing Adobe Photoshop, the aim being to imitate the feel of the in-game interface. This also extends to the aesthetic of the user interaction with the application, such as drag-and-drop functionality for manipulating the cards. The game will also feature AI agents that act as opponents to the player, if the player chooses to play against AI, and networking functionality for matches between real players.
